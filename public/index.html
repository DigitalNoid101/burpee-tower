Burpee: boost + launch
Quick jump

Finish a burpee (↓ ↓ ↑ ↑) and the star jump launches you, with a flip. Chain burpees to build boost and launch higher each time. Chain burpees to climb the boost ranks (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Legend), but the higher you go the faster the stopwatch drains. Boost fades when it runs out, or when you drop off a block. Space is a quick jump that uses your current boost. P pauses, M mutes.

Swipe down, down, up, up anywhere on the screen to burpee and launch. In mid-air, hold the left or right side of the screen to move that way (or tilt your phone). Use the Buttons toggle to show the on-screen arrows and JUMP, or hide them and just tap the screen for a quick jump. Chain burpees to build boost before the stopwatch runs out.
